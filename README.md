## 🛰️ 6-Stop Spatial Narrative & Editorial Architecture

Inspired by the celestial descent in *The Atelier New York*, the experience is divided into six distinct spatial stops connected by smooth depth descent, self-drawing constellation filaments, and real-time interaction:

- **STOP I // THE INTRODUCTION (`#intro`)**: 
  The primary celestial core sphere forms out of deep space. Identity statement (*"I build things that connect intelligence to the real world"*), tactile ripple cue, and minimal navigation.
- **STOP II // HOW I THINK (`#thinking`)**: 
  Pinned 3D storytelling sequence. The core expands into a tri-domain constellation (AI & Data, Software Engineering, Embedded Systems & IoT), smoothly orienting toward active nodal hubs as the visitor scrubs through stages.
- **STOP III // THE SYSTEMS (`#systems`)**: 
  The particle constellation reorganizes into an undulating technical waveform and architectural grid framing two featured production systems:
  - **VoltVision**: IoT electrical power telemetry system (PZEM-004T + ESP32) with live simulated AC waveform monitor. First Place Winner, IEEE Maharashtra Section EIG Business Pitch Competition (Aug 2026).
  - **File2Base**: 3-stage visual transformation pipeline converting loose CSV/Excel files into structured relational SQLite schemas.
- **STOP IV // THE EXPERIMENTS (`#experiments`)**: 
  The 3D space disperses into 3 distinct orbital test benches:
  - **Smart Plant Monitoring & Irrigation**: Closed-loop capacitive sensing & relay actuation (KVS National Science Exhibition representation & Regional 2nd Place).
  - **DOOMSDAY PROTOCOL**: LAN-based multi-terminal escape-room engine with 4 synchronized client stations, Flask host, and SQLite event logger.
  - **Carbon Emissions Tracker**: Regional electricity and transport emission factor calculator (CEA standards).
- **STOP V // THE PROOF (`#proof`)**: 
  The celestial structure organizes into concentric gyroscopic orbit rings framing verified institutional proof:
  - IEEE Maharashtra Section EIG Business Pitch — 1st Place (Aug 2026)
  - KVS Regional Science Exhibition — 2nd Place & National Representation
  - Head of Student Council — 40–50 students governed (Apr 2024 – Feb 2025)
  - Atal Tinkering Lab (ATL) Instructor — 1.5 years of hardware & robotics instruction
  - B.Sc. Artificial Intelligence at SAII, Symbiosis International University, Pune
- **STOP VI // OPEN CHANNEL (`#channel`)**: 
  The system resolves into a calm central sphere encircled by a tilted **Saturn bead ring** (420 orbiting particles). Direct dialogue channels, verified LinkedIn link, copyable placeholders for GitHub and email, and a client transmission console.

## 🌌 Atelier New York 3D Interactiveness

Directly translated from `theateliernewyork.com` into React Three Fiber:

1. **Interactive Click Ripple (`pressRipple`)**:
   - Clicking or tapping anywhere on the canvas (excluding clickable UI controls) sends an expanding radial shockwave through the 2,800-particle core.
   - Particles are displaced outward along their wave normal and flash with luminous ivory/gold warmth before returning to their orbit positions.
2. **Deep Celestial Starfield with Pointer Proximity Warmth**:
   - 1,600 multi-depth stars distributed through the background.
   - Pointer proximity calculation: stars near the cursor dynamically swell and brighten with warm ivory glow.
3. **Cosmic Spatial Descent on Scroll**:
   - Camera Y and Z coordinates smoothly descend through space as the visitor scrolls, providing the sensation of journeying downward through the Atelier chambers.
4. **Periodic Shooting Stars / Meteors**:
   - Quiet, hairline meteor streaks periodically cross the upper and lower depth field.
5. **Connective Constellation Filaments**:
   - 180 dynamic line segments connect nearest-neighbor nodes, tracing the shape of the technical structures.
6. **Saturn Bead Ring**:
   - An elliptical ring of orbiting particles encircles the resolution sphere in Stop VI, subtly responding to cursor parallax.
7. **Atelier Roman HUD & Live Pulse Counter**:
   - Floating vertical rail with Roman numerals `[ I · II · III · IV · V · VI ]` connected by a continuous thread, complete with a live pulse counter.
